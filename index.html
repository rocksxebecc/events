💡 Frequently Asked Questions

🎟️ How do I check in at an event? Go to the My Tickets section (or click "My Ticket" on any enrolled event card) to open your digital pass. Show the QR code to event checkpoint coordinators.
💼 How do 1-on-1 mentorship bookings work? Navigate to the Career section, browse appointed campus mentors, pick an available slot, and submit your topic. Your appointment will be confirmed immediately.
✏️ What if my Roll Number or Department is incorrect? Update your details under the Personal Details tab above and click "Save Details". All new passes and rosters will automatically reflect your updated details.

📩 Contact Campus Support Desk

Need help with ticket passes, event access, or technical issues? Send a direct message to the campus IT support team.
